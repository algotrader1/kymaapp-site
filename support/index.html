Kyma — Support

Questions, bug reports, refund requests or anything else — write to support@kymaapp.com. We answer every message.

Subscriptions & billing

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. To view, change or cancel a plan: open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use this direct link. Cancelling there stops all future charges immediately; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

Refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple declines and you believe the charge was a mistake, email us and we will help you appeal.

Restoring a purchase

New device, or reinstalled the app? Open Kyma and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Health data

Kyma can read Heart Rate Variability from Apple Health if you grant permission, and this requires an Apple Watch — iPhone has no HRV sensor. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Kyma. Revoking it does not affect audio playback.

Audio & headphones

Binaural tracks need stereo separation to work — use headphones or earbuds. Other frequency types play correctly on speakers.
